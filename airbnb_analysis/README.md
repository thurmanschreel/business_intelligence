Findings
1. Hosts with ten or more listings hold 31.90% of Chicago's listings, showing that a substantial share of the market is concentrated among larger hosts.

2. Columbus has the lowest median price per person among qualifying neighborhoods at North Linden, where the median is $32.55 per person.

3. The median estimated annual revenue for entire homes in the Twin Cities is $19,619, showing the typical revenue level for this room type.

4. The top 10% of Columbus listings account for 36.08% of the city's estimated revenue, showing that revenue is concentrated among a relatively small share of listings.

5. August 2025 was the busiest month for Twin Cities reviews, with 7,399 reviews, which was 2.37 times the number in its quietest month.
